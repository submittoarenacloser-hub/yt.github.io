Privacy Policy

YouTube Tools · last updated 17 November 2026

Who uses the app

YouTube Tools is a private desktop utility. It runs only on its owner's computer and only the owner signs in, to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. There is no public sign-up and no service for other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. Its use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • the signed-in channel's ID and title, to check that a video goes to the right channel;
  • the videos, thumbnails, captions and metadata that the owner uploads with the app;
  • the channel's YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting data, such as views, watch time, audience retention, impressions and click-through rate.

How the data is stored and used

  • Everything stays on the owner's computer. The app has no server and sends data only to Google's YouTube APIs.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in a local folder on that computer.
  • Analytics reports are saved as local files for the owner's own use.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app does not use cookies or tracking.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access and data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local folder deletes the stored tokens and reports.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
