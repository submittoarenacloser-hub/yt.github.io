YouTube Tools

YouTube Tools is a private desktop utility that a channel owner runs on their own computer. It does two things for the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • uploads the owner's finished videos as private, together with their title, description, thumbnail and captions;
  • reads the owner's channel and video analytics (views, watch time, retention, impressions) and saves a report on the same computer.

It has no server, no accounts for other people and no public sign-up. Only the owner signs in with their Google account.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
